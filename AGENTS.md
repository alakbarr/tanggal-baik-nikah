### 2.2 The Human Voice Canon

1. **The Uncheckable Test:**  
   AI Slop features smooth sentences devoid of checkable facts. Every explanatory paragraph must cite primary manuscripts (Betaljemur chapter numbers), neptu values, or verified Hadith numbers. If a paragraph contains only generic praise without data ("this system is comprehensive and sophisticated"), **delete that paragraph immediately**.
2. **Sentence Burstiness:**  
   AI generates monotonous 15–20 word sentences. Human writing varies dynamically:
   - Punchy short sentences for decisive facts: *"The result is zero."*, *"This rule is absolute."*
   - Medium causal sentences.
   - Longer structured sentences providing complete context.
3. **Active Voice & Explicit Subjects:**  
   Avoid omitting agents and avoid heavy nominalization (*"an optimization of the algorithm was performed by the system"* $\to$ *"We optimized the search algorithm"*).
4. **Scholarly & Culturally Respectful Tone (Without Mystification):**  
   - Javanese Adat: Use authentic terminology (*weton, neptu, padewan, sangar, wuku, asapon*) with philological precision, avoiding occult mystification.
   - Islamic Shariah: Use exact fiqh terms (*rukun, valid conditions, mubah, makruh, thiyarah, taysir, istikharah*) with verified citations (Bukhari, Muslim, Tirmidhi).
5. **The Read-Aloud Test:**  
   Read text internally before output. If any phrase sounds like corporate marketing brochures, motivational seminars, or machine translation, rewrite it completely.

### 4.1 Calendrical Formulas
- **Julian Day Number (JDN):** Standard Gregorian Fliegel-Van Flandern algorithm.
- **Day of Week (Saptawara):** `(JDN + 1) % 7` (0=Sunday, 1=Monday, ..., 6=Saturday).
- **Market Day (Pancawara):** `JDN % 5` (0=Legi, 1=Pahing, 2=Pon, 3=Wage, 4=Kliwon).
- **Wuku (Pawukon 30 Wuku):** `wuku_index = Math.floor((JDN - 146) / 7) % 30` (0=Sinta, ..., 29=Watugunung).
- **Day Neptu:** Sunday=5, Monday=4, Tuesday=3, Wednesday=7, Thursday=8, Friday=6, Saturday=9.
- **Market Neptu:** Legi=5, Pahing=9, Pon=7, Wage=4, Kliwon=8.
- **Pawukon day position:** `d = (JDN - 146) mod 210` (d=0 is Sunday of wuku Sinta). Sadwara/Paringkelan uses `d mod 6`, never `JDN mod 6` (the offsets differ by 2).
- **Hastawara/Padewan (8) and Sangawara/Padangon (9)** do not divide 210, so they carry irregular repeats (*Kala tiga*, *Dangu pat*). Implement only from a transcribed rule with test anchors; `JDN mod 8` is wrong.
- **Javanese year names:** `(tahunJawa - 1867) mod 8` → 0=Alip, 1=Ehe, 2=Jimawal, 3=Je, 4=Dal, 5=Be, 6=Wawu, 7=Jimakir. Long years (355 days, 30-day Besar): Ehe, Dal, Jimakir.

### 4.2 Canonical Javanese Systems
Couple-profile rules (depend only on the couple) and date rules (depend on the candidate date) are separate layers. Couple-profile results are shown in their own panel and must never change the ranking of dates.

1. **Rule No. 15 Betaljemur (Pasatohan 9x9)** — couple profile:
   - 2D coordinate `(groom neptu mod 9, bride neptu mod 9)`, remainder 0 → 9. Never `(groom + bride) mod 9`.
   - Cell contents come only from the transcription in `RESEARCH.md`; until all 81 cells are sourced the rule stays `dataStatus: 'pending'`.
2. **Rule No. 34 Betaljemur (3-Table Day Selection)** — date rule:
   - Keyed by weekday and its N-th occurrence in the Javanese month. Optimum: all 3 tables agree on a favourable outcome (*tumbuk tibané padha becik*).
3. **Popular 8-category couple table (Pegat … Pesthi)** — couple profile:
   - Formula: `r = (groom neptu + bride neptu) mod 9`; r ∈ {0, 1} → *Pegat*, 2=*Ratu*, 3=*Jodoh*, 4=*Topo*, 5=*Tinari*, 6=*Padu*, 7=*Sujanan*, 8=*Pesthi*.
   - Proof: this reproduces all 35 entries of the published table (Pegat 9, 10, 18, 19, 27, 28, 36; Ratu 2, 11, 20, 29; …). The earlier `mod 8` formula matched only 8 of 35. Example: 15 + 13 = 28 → *Pegat*.
4. **Pati Field Tradition (Setiaji, Undip; manuscript of Desa Kropak, Winong, Pati)** — date rule:
   - `(groom neptu + bride neptu + wedding-day neptu) mod 3 == 2`. Active only in the "Pati / Pesisiran" tradition profile.
5. **Adat pantangan (date filters, enabled per tradition profile):**
   - *Kunarpaning Warsa* (last day of Besar), *Sangaring Warsa* (3 Sura), *Bangas Padewan*, monthly *Taliwangke*.
   - Personal: wedding day equal to either spouse's birth weton, a parent's birth weton, a deceased parent's *geblak* weton, or a spouse's *dina pengapesan* (count birth neptu days inclusively from the birth weton).
   - Label these "Pantangan adat". Never use fiqh words such as *haram* for customary taboos.

### 4.3 Islamic Shariah Foundation (Anti-Tathayyur & Taysir)
1. **Primacy of Monotheism (*Tawhid*):** Days and dates hold no intrinsic power to cause misfortune or bestow fortune. Attributing bad luck to a day (*thiyarah/tathayyur*) is condemned as shirk (*HR. Tirmidhi 1614, Abu Dawud 3910; Bukhari 5757, Muslim 2220*).
2. **Status of Petungan:** Cultural wisdom (*'urf*) used to reach family consensus, never a determinant of fate.
3. **Validity of Marriage Contract:** The 5 pillars per KHI Pasal 14: groom, bride, *wali*, two witnesses, *ijab-qabul*. *Mahr* is obligatory but is **not** a pillar (KHI Pasal 34 ayat 1). Customary date taboos have zero power to invalidate a marriage.
4. **Date-related Shariah limits (the only hard Shariah constraints on a date):** the bride's *iddah* must have ended (QS Al-Baqarah 235); neither party nor the wali may be in *ihram* (HR. Muslim 1409). Both are optional user inputs.
5. **Commended Times:** Shawwal (*HR. Muslim 1423*) is a direct Prophetic precedent. Friday is the best day of the week (*HR. Muslim 854*); holding the akad on Friday is a scholarly recommendation by analogy, not a specific Sunnah. Word the UI accordingly.
6. **The Taysir Principle:** Weekends ease attendance for guests. This is *maslahah* (public benefit), not a Sunnah, and must be labelled as a practical preference.
